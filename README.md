## SetupSavvy

This is a [Next.js](https://nextjs.org) project bootstrapped with [`create-next-app`](https://nextjs.org/docs/app/api-reference/cli/create-next-app).

SetupSavvy is a tech lifestyle website that focuses on helping users optimize their work-from-home setups, gaming rigs, and productivity environments. Here’s a brief overview:
www.setupsavvy.in

- *Product Reviews & Recommendations* – Detailed lists of the best gadgets, peripherals, and accessories (headsets, keyboards, monitors, chairs, etc.) with pros, cons, and price ranges.

- *Buying Guides* – Step-by-step advice on how to choose the right gear based on comfort, performance, and budget.

- *Setup Inspiration* – Showcase of ergonomic and aesthetic desk setups for work, gaming, or content creation.

- *Tech Tips & How-Tos* – Guides on improving workflow, cable management, and optimizing device performance.

Essentially, SetupSavvy is about helping users make informed purchasing decisions and create efficient, stylish, and functional setups.
